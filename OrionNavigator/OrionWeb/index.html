About Orion

Empowering Independence Through Smart Technology

Orion is a smart navigation application designed specifically for visually impaired users. Combining BLE beacon technology, AI camera, and real-time location sharing, Orion provides a comprehensive solution for safe and independent indoor navigation.

Where Orion Lives

Tested & Deployed at YPAB Surabaya

Orion's navigation system is designed and calibrated specifically for YPAB Gebang Putih — a school for visually impaired students in Surabaya. Every room, corridor, and pathway has been mapped with BLE beacons.

Yayasan Pendidikan Anak Buta (YPAB)

SLB-A Gebang Putih Surabaya

YPAB Gebang Putih is a special school (SLB-A) dedicated to educating visually impaired children in Surabaya. The school provides a supportive learning environment with various programs including academics, music (gamelan), scouting (pramuka), and life skills. Orion was developed and tested here, with all navigation features precisely mapped to every room and facility within the school grounds.

Features

Everything You Need,
All in One App

Orion combines cutting-edge technology to provide a seamless navigation experience for visually impaired users.

BLE Beacon Navigation

Navigate indoors effortlessly using Bluetooth Low Energy beacons. Get real-time distance estimation and turn-by-turn audio guidance with compass and step counter integration.

📡 BLE Technology

AI Camera

Powered by Gemini AI, the camera identifies objects around you and provides descriptive audio feedback. Point your phone and let Orion describe what it sees.

🤖 Gemini AI

Live Location Sharing

Share your real-time location securely with caregivers using a simple pairing code. Parents and companions can monitor location on an interactive map for peace of mind.

📍 Real-time GPS

Voice Command

Control the entire app hands-free with voice commands. Just say "Hello Orion" to activate voice control and navigate through all features effortlessly.

🎙️ Hands-free
